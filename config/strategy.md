# Current reconstruction strategy

Transfer connected source families continuously, using Android as source evidence
and the PC executable as the authority for identity, ABI, complete boundaries,
constants and control flow. Choose work by original PC bytes and real dependencies
unlocked per shared layout. Fix incorrect behavior or contracts immediately;
instruction scheduling and register tuning are secondary.

Prepare broad candidate waves, typically 50–100 KB where credible leads exist,
then release reviewed subsets. Candidate spans and inventory-only mappings earn
no reconstruction credit. Reconnect already-real providers in partial parents
alongside new source; caller restoration earns no new unique-byte credit.

Recover only the named field cohort needed by a real family, with asserted PC
widths/offsets and genuine storage/provider contracts. Unknown allocation extents
remain unknown. Preserve original helper/caller and private-state ownership where
established; do not force public wrappers over genuine private register ABIs.
A missing value-producing dependency holds its consumer; an accurately marked
omitted void action may remain in an explicitly partial reconstruction.

Matching and runnable milestones have separate evidence. The current source game
is not playable. See the [roadmap](../docs/roadmap.md) for concrete runtime goals
and [execution policy](transfer-execution.md) for verification and coordination.
Reuse retained evidence and investigate concrete discrepancies rather than
repeating whole-binary discovery or released-family audits.

The complete [2026-09-30 strategy review](../docs/history/strategy-review-2026-09-30.md)
is historical, including its baseline numbers and experiments.
